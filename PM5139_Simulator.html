The core executes the 8051 code of the PCB80C652. Timers, interrupts, the serial C-bus and the I²C bus are emulated; the display is read out of the real PCF8576 data stream, and the keys produce the pulse-width coded SAA3007 stream on P3.3. The battery-backed RAM is preloaded, the arbitrary curves in the EEPROM are generated at start-up and checked by the firmware itself. The key labelling is on assignment through the key code 0Ah - it may differ from the real front panel.
